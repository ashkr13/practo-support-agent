# Follow-up Visit Discount Policy

A follow-up visit with the same doctor for the same condition within 7 days of the original consultation is free of charge. A follow-up booked between 8 and 14 days after the original consultation receives a 50 percent discount on the consultation fee. Follow-ups booked after 14 days are charged at the full fee. The discount applies automatically when the follow-up is booked using the original appointment ID.
