# Telemedicine Eligibility

Video consultations are available for follow-ups, skin conditions, minor infections, medication reviews, and discussion of existing lab reports. Telemedicine is not suitable for chest pain, breathing difficulty, injuries that need a physical examination, or any emergency. Patients need a stable internet connection and a device with a camera to join a video consultation. A doctor may end a video consultation and ask the patient to visit in person if a physical examination is required.
