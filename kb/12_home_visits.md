# Home Visit Eligibility

Home visits are available for patients over 65 years of age, patients with limited mobility, and patients recovering from surgery within the last 30 days. Home visits cover General Medicine, Pediatrics, and post-operative Orthopedics care, but not Cardiology or Dermatology. A home visit must be booked at least 24 hours in advance and carries a travel charge of 500 rupees in addition to the consultation fee. Home visits are offered only within 15 kilometres of a partner clinic.
