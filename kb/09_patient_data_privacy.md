# Patient Data Privacy Policy

Patient health records, prescriptions, and contact details are stored encrypted and are shared only with the treating doctor and the clinic involved in the patient's care. Practo never sells patient data and shares it with insurers or third parties only with the patient's explicit consent. Patients can download or request deletion of their records from the privacy settings page at any time. Support staff will never ask a patient for a password or one-time code over the phone.
