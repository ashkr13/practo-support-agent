# Cancellation and Rescheduling Window

An appointment can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours before the slot begins. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the slot forfeit 50 percent of the consultation fee. Each appointment may be rescheduled at most twice, after which it must be cancelled and booked again. Patients who miss an appointment without cancelling are marked as a No-Show and receive no refund.
