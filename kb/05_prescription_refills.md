# Prescription Refill Policy

A prescription refill can be requested through the app for ongoing medicines that the same doctor prescribed within the last 90 days. The doctor reviews each refill request within one working day and may approve it, adjust the dose, or ask the patient to book a consultation. Refills are never issued for controlled substances or antibiotics without a fresh consultation. Approved refill prescriptions are sent digitally and can be used at any registered pharmacy.
