# Lab Test Turnaround Times

Routine blood tests such as a complete blood count or lipid profile report within 24 hours of sample collection. Specialised tests such as thyroid panels and vitamin levels take 48 to 72 hours, while culture and biopsy reports can take up to 7 days. Reports are uploaded to the patient's Practo health record and an SMS alert is sent when they are ready. If a report is delayed beyond the stated time, patients can raise a ticket from the lab order page.
