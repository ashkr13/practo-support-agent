# Insurance Claim Process

Patients with health insurance can request a cashless claim at partner clinics by sharing their policy card at the reception desk before the consultation. For non-partner clinics, patients pay upfront and file a reimbursement claim with their insurer using the itemised invoice and prescription that Practo emails after the visit. Practo provides these documents within 24 hours but does not decide whether a claim is approved, which remains the insurer's decision. Outpatient consultations are covered only if the patient's policy includes outpatient benefits.
