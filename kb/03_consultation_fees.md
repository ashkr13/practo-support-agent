# Consultation Fee Structure by Specialty

Consultation fees depend on the specialty and the seniority of the doctor. General Medicine consultations cost between 300 and 700 rupees, Pediatrics between 400 and 900 rupees, and Dermatology between 500 and 1,200 rupees. Orthopedics consultations cost between 600 and 1,500 rupees, while Cardiology consultations cost between 800 and 2,000 rupees. The exact fee is shown before booking and is paid in advance online or at the clinic counter.
