# Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can book an in-clinic appointment through the Practo app, the website, or by calling the clinic front desk. Bookings open up to 30 days in advance and close 2 hours before the chosen slot. A booking is confirmed only after the patient receives an SMS and in-app confirmation carrying an appointment ID such as APT-1001. Patients should arrive 10 minutes early, because a slot is held for only 15 minutes past its start time.
